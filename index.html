Sleep at anchor with an extra pair of eyes.

AnchorGood watches your boat's GPS position while you are at anchor and sounds a loud alarm on your iPhone if the boat leaves the circle you set.

Free · No account · No ads · No tracking

Coming soon to the App Store for iPhone.

Drop anchor in five steps

Check GPS readiness, mark where the anchor went down, enter depth and rode, confirm the suggested alarm radius and arm the watch.

A clear, loud alarm

Alarm sound with vibration and a Time Sensitive notification. Hold to acknowledge — monitoring continues. A GPS-lost warning when the position cannot be trusted.

Keeps watching

Works with the screen locked and in the background, and without internet — only the weather needs a connection.

Weather for your anchorage

Wind, gusts, waves and swell for the next hours, with arrows showing where the wind pushes the boat.

Night Watch

A dark display with warm, dim text that protects your night vision.

Your data stays on your phone

No account and no cloud. Call, text or WhatsApp your safety contacts in one tap from the alarm screen — you send the message.

Important

AnchorGood is an extra pair of eyes, not a replacement for a proper anchor watch, official charts or good seamanship. It needs “Always” location and the phone should stay charging during a watch. If the app is swiped away or the phone switches off, the watch stops.

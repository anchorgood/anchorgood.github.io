Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-21

Who we are

AnchorGood is published by an independent developer ("we"). Questions about this policy or your data: anchorgoodco@gmail.com. AnchorGood sends no personal data to us, so we hold none about you.

What stays on your phone

AnchorGood has no account and does not send your data to an AnchorGood server. What you enter or record is stored on this phone: your boat and anchor details, safety contacts, anchor watches with their GPS track, alarms and settings. We cannot see or restore this data.

Detailed GPS tracks of finished watches are deleted from the phone after the history period you choose in Settings (14 days by default), the next time the app opens. Deleting the app deletes all of this data from the phone; copies can remain in your own iCloud or computer backups of the phone until those backups are replaced.

What leaves your phone

Weather: to show wind, gusts and sea state, the phone asks Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com and marine-api.open-meteo.com) for a forecast at your position rounded to about 1 km. This happens when you open Home, the Map or Weather, while you drop anchor, and about every few minutes while the app is open. No name, contact or anchor watch is sent. Like any internet request, it carries your IP address and the phone’s standard request information.

Sea marks on the map: when the map is shown with the "OpenSeaMap seamarks" layer on (the default), the phone downloads map tiles for the area on screen from tiles.openseamap.org. The tiles requested reveal the area you are looking at, which during an anchor watch is around your boat, together with your IP address. You can turn this layer off in the map layers.

Depth shading: if you turn on the "Bathymetry (EMODnet)" map layer (off by default), the phone downloads map tiles for the area on screen from tiles.emodnet-bathymetry.eu, together with your IP address.

Base map: map images and place names come from Apple Maps (MapKit). Apple receives the map area being shown, under Apple’s privacy policy.

Messages you send: when you tap Call, SMS, WhatsApp, E-mail or Share on the alarm screen, your phone opens that app with a prepared text that may contain your boat name and exact position. Nothing is sent unless you send it; the message is then handled by that app or service.

These services are run by independent providers (Open-Meteo, OpenSeaMap, EMODnet and Apple), some of them outside your country, so these requests may be processed abroad. They receive only what is described above, never your name or an identifier from AnchorGood. We use them because their published terms protect this data at least as described in this policy.

AnchorGood contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting service.

Permissions

Location (precise, and "Always" so the watch keeps running when the screen is locked) is used to watch your anchor, to show your position and to fetch the weather. Positions are recorded only during an anchor watch. Notifications are used for alarms. You can change both in iOS Settings; without location the anchor watch cannot run.

Legal basis

Data is processed to provide the anchor watch you asked for and for your and your crew’s safety. Location and notifications are used only with your permission in iOS.

How long data is kept

On your phone, detailed tracks are not copied in any way during the history period you choose. The weather and map services above keep their own request logs under their own policies.

Your rights

Your data is on your phone and under your control: you can change or delete it in the app, or delete the app. For questions write to anchorgoodco@gmail.com. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in Türkiye: KVKK; in the EU: your national authority).

Children

AnchorGood is not intended for children.

Changes

If this policy changes we show the new version in the app and ask you to accept it. Anchor monitoring is never blocked by this.

Telefonunuzdan çıkanlar

Hava durumu: rüzgârı, hamleyi ve deniz durumunu göstermek için telefon, yaklaşık 1 km’ye yuvarlanmış konumunuz için Open-Meteo’dan (api.open-meteo.com ve marine-api.open-meteo.com) tahmin ister. Haritadaki deniz işaretleri: harita "OpenSeaMap deniz işaretleri" katmanı açıkken (varsayılan) gösterildiğinde telefon, ekrandaki bölgenin harita karolarını tiles.openseamap.org’dan indirir. Derinlik gösterimi: "Derinlik (EMODnet)" harita katmanını açarsanız (varsayılan kapalı) telefon, ekrandaki bölgenin harita karolarını IP adresinizle birlikte tiles.emodnet-bathymetry.eu’dan indirir.

Temel harita: harita görüntüleri ve yer adları Apple Haritalar’dan (MapKit) gelir. Apple, gösterilen harita bölgesini kendi gizlilik politikası kapsamında alır.

AnchorGood nedir

AnchorGood yalnızca bilgilendirme ve uyarı amaçlı yardımcı bir araçtır. Seyir sistemi, harita plotterı, ECDIS ya da resmi deniz haritası değildir. Resmi deniz haritalarının, sertifikalı harita plotterlarının ve birincil seyir cihazlarının, bir kişinin tuttuğu düzgün bir demir nöbetinin, iyi denizciliğin ya da kaptanın kendi muhakemesinin yerini tutmaz ve teknenin güvenliği için tek araç olarak kullanılmamalıdır.

GPS, hava durumu, harita, telefon, pil, bildirimler ve internet çalışmayabilir, gecikebilir ya da hatalı olabilir. Alarm yalnızca izleyen telefonda çalar: onu şarjda ve duyabileceğiniz bir yerde tutun. O telefon susarsa başka kimseye haber gitmez.
